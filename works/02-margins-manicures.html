I Saw A Fresco In A TV Show, Is It Real?

essay · 2024

A comedic-but-researched dive into the anime Arte, using it as a case study for how historical fiction negotiates truth versus "the real." Drawing on historians like Hayden White and Margaret King, the essay checks the show's Renaissance details: tempera prep, pigment economics, fresco painting, patronage, guild life, against the historical record, landing on a playful defense of pop culture as a legitimate (if imperfect) entry point into history.
