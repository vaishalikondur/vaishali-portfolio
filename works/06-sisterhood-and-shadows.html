Sisterhood and Shadows: Women Who Definitely Tried to Haunt Your Dreams

Thesis · 2026

A supposedly academic essay on Greta Gerwig's Little Women, and Anvita Dutt's Bulbbul, written with the seriousness of someone who knows exactly how many marks are riding on it. What starts as a comparison of two films directed by women spirals into tangents, dramatic overstatements, and a narrator who cannot be trusted to stay on topic for more than a paragraph at a time. Expect commentary on womanhood, folklore, sisterhood, and the general chaos of watching two emotionally intense films back-to-back — plus the occasional aside about Barbie, because some things simply must be mentioned. Read at your own risk; rigor was attempted, but humor won.
