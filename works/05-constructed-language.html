Constructed Language

language design · 2024

What happens when you approach language not as something you inherit, but as something you deliberately build? For this project, I designed a constructed language (ConLang) from the ground up as a part of a course in Morphology. The aim was to create more than a collection of invented words: the language needed to function as a coherent system, with its own grammar, vocabulary, and underlying patterns. The project allowed me to take concepts from linguistic theory and experiment with them creatively, making deliberate choices about how sounds, words, and sentences interact within a language. The result is a small but fully structured linguistic system, documented here.
