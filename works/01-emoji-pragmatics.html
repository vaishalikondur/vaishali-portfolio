Dakhini Urdu: A Comparative Study

comparative study · 2022

Intrigued by how the same language can take on different forms through different linguistic and cultural lenses, this paper examines Dakhini Urdu as spoken in Hyderabad and Bangalore in comparison with Standard Urdu/Hindi. Through native-speaker examples, it traces regional differences in vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and sentence structure, while examining the influence of local languages, particularly Telugu, on Dakhini Urdu. The study demonstrates how language evolves with its speakers and surroundings, producing distinct regional identities while remaining mutually intelligible.
