Age Based Variations in Emoji Pragmatics: Interpretation and Intergenerational Miscommunication

Thesis · 2026

This dissertation explores how emoji meanings vary across generations and how these differences contribute to digital miscommunication. Using survey data from 70 participants across four age groups, the study examines how context, sender age, platform, and individual communication habits shape emoji interpretation. The findings reveal a clear generational divide: younger users are more likely to interpret emoji through irony and sarcasm, while older users tend toward more literal meanings. Relationship context emerges as the strongest influence on interpretation, while over half of participants report experiencing emoji-related misunderstandings. Grounded in a multilingual Indian context, the research demonstrates how seemingly universal digital symbols can carry radically different meanings depending on who is using—and interpreting—them.
