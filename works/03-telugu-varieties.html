Telugu: A Russian Doll of Dialects

comparative study · 2024

This is a comparative study built from a 50-word wordlist across varieties of Telugu, namely the Telangana, Andhra, and the "Bangalore" or Chittoor variety of the Telugu language, examining regional linguistic variation and diversity. This study represents interest in understanding similar phenomena across geographical area, connected by the same roots.
